> The builders' self-conception is one of finitude and limitation. The builder is constantly experiencing [the resistance of the real](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2018-08-29/miami-s-other-water-problem?ref=sonyasupposedly.com) and presence of the gaze. Every wall and tower is meant to stand but is also understood in its being as a tenuous thing. Unlike the swarm, it constitutes a thing in space and time. The appreciation of ruins is the appreciation of the nature of these constructions. The builders struggle to make a society which can provide safety. But they understand that this is possible only insofar as it is allowed by God. Eventually all things are destined to pass out of this abode.
