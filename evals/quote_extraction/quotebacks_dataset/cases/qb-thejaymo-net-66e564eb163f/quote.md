> # ABOUT THE GUEST
>
> MIKE RUGNETTA is Brookyn-based writer, producer, and sound designer. He is the co-creator and host of [Never Post](https://neverpo.st/), a podcast about the internet – as well as the co-creator, co-DM and sound designer of the actual play tabletop roleplaying podcast [Fun City](https://funcity.ventures/). Previously he was the writer, researcher and host of the multiple-award winning PBS Digital series Idea Channel, and the host of Crash Courses *Theater and Dramaturgy* and *World Mythology*. His production clients have included Atlas Obscura, Adobe, Duolingo, Future of Storytelling, IBM, Longreads and more.
