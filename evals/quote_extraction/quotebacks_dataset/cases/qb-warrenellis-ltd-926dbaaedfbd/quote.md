> Now, an international team of researchers claims to have used NASA’s Hubble Space Telescope to discover an entirely new type of celestial object: dubbed “Cloud-9,” it’s a “starless, gas-rich, dark-matter cloud,” [per the European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Cloud-9_a_new_celestial_object_found_by_Hubble). The lack of stars caught the team by surprise, indicating Cloud-9 was a “fossil leftover” — what [*ScienceAlert* memorably termed](https://www.sciencealert.com/mysterious-cloud-9-may-be-the-dark-matter-bones-of-a-failed-galaxy) the “dark-matter bones of a failed galaxy.”
