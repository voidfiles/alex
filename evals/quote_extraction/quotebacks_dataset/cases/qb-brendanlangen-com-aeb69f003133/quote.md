> Great software environments are enabling environments. Photoshop expands experts’ range of artistic expression and unlocks previously-rarefied photo enhancement techniques for novices. Software development tools enable teenagers to make games and distribute them to millions at zero marginal cost. By contrast, [Most games aren’t enabling environments](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/z6DCZK1JtHU3waXWTk5UbNt3kXU4WRT9EwNND), and [Educational games are a doomed approach to creating enabling environments](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/z7wPt3dxX5hp6LK3PLUBTJXxk7kAhMuh8UDck).
