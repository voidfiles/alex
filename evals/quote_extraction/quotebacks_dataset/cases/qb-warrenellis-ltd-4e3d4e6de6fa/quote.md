> An **Egregore** (also spelled **egregor**; from [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language "French language") égrégore, from [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek_language "Ancient Greek language") ἐγρήγορος, egrēgoros ‘wakeful’) is a concept in [Western esotericism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_esotericism "Western esotericism") of a [non-physical entity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-physical_entity "Non-physical entity") or [thoughtform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoughtform "Thoughtform") that arises from the collective thoughts and emotions of a distinct group of individuals.
