Great news—we’ve hit our (very modest) performance goals for the CPython JIT over a year early for macOS AArch64, and a few months early for x86\_64 Linux. The 3.15 alpha JIT is about **11-12%** faster on macOS AArch64 than the tail calling interpreter, and **5-6%**faster than the standard interpreter on x86\_64 Linux.

— Ken Jin
